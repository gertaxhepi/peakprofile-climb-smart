# PeakProfile

PeakProfile is an AI-assisted mountaineering platform that helps climbers understand whether they are ready for a specific expedition.

It turns a mountaineer’s experience, skills and completed routes into a structured profile. This profile is compared with expedition requirements to produce an explainable readiness assessment, highlight areas for improvement and suggest suitable next objectives.

## How it works

1. A mountain guide publishes an expedition.
2. The mountaineer completes their climbing profile.
3. PeakProfile compares the profile with the route requirements.
4. The mountaineer receives an explainable readiness assessment.
5. The guide reviews the result and makes the final decision.

## Product approach

PeakProfile combines structured data and deterministic safety rules with AI-generated explanations. AI supports the decision-making process but does not replace the professional judgement of a qualified mountain guide.

## Status

Currently an early-stage product prototype, developed and validated with feedback from mountaineers and mountain guides.
